About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Sejong University, Seoul. My research focuses on language-driven multimodal generation and embodied AI, including instruction-driven 3D facial expression generation and language-grounded navigation in 3D environments.

Before my PhD, I was a lecturer at the Faculty of Information Technology, Ton Duc Thang University, Vietnam (2012–2023). I am currently looking for postdoctoral positions starting in 2027 — feel free to reach out.
