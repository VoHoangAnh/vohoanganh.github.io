PhD Candidate · Computer Science

Anh H. Vo

Võ Hoàng Anh

PhD Program in Computer Science, Sejong University

Seoul, Republic of Korea · Advisor: Prof. Yong-Guk Kim

My research focuses on language-driven multimodal generation and embodied AI, including instruction-driven 3D facial expression generation, sign language production, and language-grounded navigation in 3D environments. Before my PhD, I was a lecturer at the Faculty of Information Technology, Ton Duc Thang University, Vietnam (2012–2023).

I am looking for postdoctoral positions starting in 2028 — feel free to reach out.
